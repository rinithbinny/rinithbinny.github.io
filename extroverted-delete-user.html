ACCOUNT & DATA

Account Deletion

Last updated: October 14, 2026

At Extroverted, we believe you should have control over your account and personal information. If you no longer want to use the App, you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

Prefer the fastest option? If the account deletion option is available inside the App, you can use it directly. If you cannot access the App, you can contact us by email and we will help process your request.

How to Delete Your Account

To request deletion directly from Extroverted, follow these steps:

1
Open the Extroverted app.
2
Open your Profile or account settings.
3
Find the Delete Account option.
4
Follow the confirmation steps and confirm that you want to delete your account.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Deletion is intended to be permanent

Once your deletion request has been processed, deleted account data generally cannot be recovered.

Depending on the information associated with your account, deletion may remove or permanently anonymize information such as:

  • Your account and profile information.
  • Your lesson or chapter progress associated with the account.
  • Saved preferences or other account-related information.
  • Other information that we associate directly with your account and that is eligible for deletion.

Some information may be retained where required by law, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or satisfy legitimate legal or accounting obligations.

Camera & Microphone Data

Extroverted uses the camera and microphone for certain interactive exercises and levels. We do not permanently store camera or microphone recordings on our servers.

Camera and microphone-related information may temporarily exist in the App's local cache while a feature is being used. Deleting your account does not change the fact that we do not maintain permanent server-side recordings of this data.

AI-Processed Responses

Some exercise responses may be processed by Google Gemini so that Extroverted can analyze and score your answers.

Account deletion does not necessarily cause third-party services to immediately delete information they may independently process or retain. Such information is subject to the applicable third-party provider's policies and service configuration.

Subscriptions & Purchases

Deleting the Extroverted account or uninstalling the App does not necessarily cancel an active subscription.

If you purchased a monthly or yearly subscription through Apple App Store or Google Play, manage or cancel the subscription through the relevant app-store subscription settings.

Important: Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions. Deleting your account does not automatically guarantee cancellation of future subscription billing.

Can't Access the App?

If you have uninstalled Extroverted, lost access to your account, or cannot use the in-app deletion option, you can send us a deletion request by email.
