PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Extroverted handles information when you use our app. We designed the app to help you build communication and social-confidence skills while keeping the information needed to operate the experience as limited as practical.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Extroverted mobile application and related services (collectively, the “App”). In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of Extroverted.

By using Extroverted, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

In short: Extroverted uses your device’s camera and microphone for certain interactive lessons and levels. We do not store camera or microphone recordings on our servers. Certain responses may be sent to Google Gemini so that our service can analyze and score your answers.

2. Information We Collect

The information handled by Extroverted depends on how you use the App. This may include:

📷
Camera data Used during applicable exercises and levels. Camera input is not stored by us as a permanent recording.
🎙️
Microphone data Used during applicable speaking or interactive exercises. We do not store microphone recordings on our servers.
🤖
Exercise responses Responses may be processed by Google Gemini to evaluate and score answers.
📱
App & purchase information Information needed to operate the App, subscriptions, purchases, advertising, and basic service functionality may be processed by us or our service providers.

We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information simply to use the core learning experience. Please avoid voluntarily submitting sensitive information in free-form responses unless it is necessary for an exercise.

3. Camera & Microphone

Some Extroverted levels require access to your device’s camera and/or microphone to make the experience interactive and to determine whether you have completed an exercise.

What happens to this data?

  • Camera and microphone access is used only when required by an applicable exercise.
  • We do not permanently store camera recordings or microphone recordings on our servers.
  • Camera and microphone-related information may exist temporarily in the App’s local device cache while the feature is running.
  • You can control camera and microphone permissions through your device settings.
Important: If you deny camera or microphone permission, features that require those permissions may not work, but you can continue using other parts of the App where available.

4. AI-Powered Analysis

Extroverted uses Google Gemini to analyze certain responses submitted during lessons and provide an evaluation or score.

What may be sent for analysis?

Depending on the exercise, the information sent for AI analysis may include the response you provide and contextual information necessary to evaluate that response.

Why do we use AI?

AI analysis helps Extroverted automatically evaluate answers and provide a more interactive learning experience.

Google may process information sent to its Gemini services in accordance with the applicable Google terms and privacy policies and the specific Gemini service configuration used by Extroverted.

5. Advertising

Extroverted uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in parts of the App.

AdMob and its partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, interaction information, and other information used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, depending on your platform, consent choices, and applicable settings.

Advertising behavior is primarily controlled by Google and the advertising settings available on your device and platform. Where required by applicable law, we will request the appropriate consent before using personal information for personalized advertising.

6. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

Extroverted offers optional paid subscriptions, including 1 month and 1 year subscription options.

Purchases are processed through the relevant app store, such as the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card or bank account details through the App.

The app store may provide us with purchase-related information necessary to verify your subscription, provide access to paid features, prevent fraud, and manage your entitlement.

Subscription billing, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and payment processing are subject to the applicable app store’s terms and policies.

7. How We Use Information

We may use information handled through Extroverted to:

  • Provide and operate the App and its features.
  • Enable interactive lessons that use the camera or microphone.
  • Analyze and score eligible exercise responses using AI.
  • Provide, verify, and manage subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain App security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of our services.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money.

We may share or make information available to service providers when necessary to operate Extroverted. These may include:

  • Google Gemini — for AI analysis and scoring of applicable responses.
  • Google AdMob — for advertising, measurement, and related advertising services.
  • Apple / Google Play — for processing and validating in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Other infrastructure or service providers that are reasonably necessary to operate, secure, or maintain the App.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of users, the public, or our services.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

We do not retain camera or microphone recordings as permanent server-side data. Temporary camera or microphone-related information may exist in the App’s local cache while needed by the App.

Information processed by third-party providers, such as Google services or app stores, may be retained according to their own policies and applicable service configurations.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled through the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children’s Privacy

Extroverted is not intended for children where the use of the App would be prohibited by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing.

You may also control certain permissions, advertising settings, and tracking preferences through your device and platform settings.
