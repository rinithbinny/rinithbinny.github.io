Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your SweatPass account directly from the app. Follow the simple steps below.

How to delete your account

To permanently delete your SweatPass account:

1
Open SweatPassOpen the SweatPass app and sign in to your account.
2
Open your ProfileTap Profile in the app.
3
Scroll downScroll to the bottom of the Profile page.
4
Delete your accountTap Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.
This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your account information and associated data will no longer be available through SweatPass, except where certain information may need to be retained by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or financial-record purposes.

What happens when you delete your account?

Your SweatPass account will be deleted and you will no longer be able to access it. Data associated with your account may be removed or anonymized as part of the deletion process.

Workout activity and other account-related information associated with your profile may no longer be recoverable after deletion.

Camera & microphone data

SweatPass may use your device camera and microphone for features that require them. We do not permanently store camera or microphone recordings on our servers. Data may temporarily exist in your device's local app cache while a feature is being used.

Subscriptions & purchases

Deleting your SweatPass account does not necessarily cancel an active subscription purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play.

If you have an active subscription, manage or cancel it separately through the store where you purchased it to prevent future charges.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account or are unable to delete it from the app, contact us and include the email address associated with your SweatPass account.
